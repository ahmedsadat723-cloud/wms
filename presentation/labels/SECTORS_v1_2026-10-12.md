## v09 — القطاعات الستة (يمين/يسار) · Six sectors (Right/Left)

## v12 — فئات الارتفاع الثلاث · Three height classes
Required clearance = pallet height + 15 cm (pallet base). 246 cm racks → pallets ≤231 (cartons 230 ✓); 195 → ≤180 (starch, zip bags, plastic); 175 → ≤160 on upper levels (floor level A up to 180, to verify on site). Starch goes with spices, zip bags with packaging. Open: pallet counts for starch / zip bags / tall plastic.
